Super Clock for Android — Privacy Policy

Draft for release review — not yet effective

Super Clock for Android is provided by ZuYuan Zhou, using the developer name Moonveil Apps. If you have questions about privacy, contact tecentmoon.feedback@gmail.com.

Clock settings and content. Your clock selection, favorites, display settings and focus-session state are stored in the app's storage on your device. If you choose a background image, the app copies it into its private storage for display. You do not need to create a Super Clock account. Device backups, if enabled, are managed through your device's settings and services.

Saving and sharing. You can choose to save or share an image through the Android system. The service or app you select handles the exported image under its own terms. Removing Super Clock does not remove copies you exported separately.

Device features. The app uses the device's time, battery information and motion sensors to provide clock displays and effects, and can keep the screen on. The app can request notification permission for focus reminders; declining it does not prevent the basic clock from working. An optional system setting can allow more accurate reminders; battery and system restrictions can affect delivery. Your selected system speech engine handles spoken announcements and may use a network depending on its own settings.

Google Play and purchases. If Pro purchases are available and you choose to purchase or redeem one, Google Play handles the transaction. The app uses Google Play Billing to receive purchase results, acknowledge purchases, and find purchases available to restore. We do not operate a separate purchase-verification server for this Android version. Google processes purchases under the Google Privacy Policy. The Billing library also includes technical logging about API success and failure and service connections to help Google improve reliability and support errors. Its transport component can attach device and Android build information, app version, language and country settings, time zone, network type and mobile-network operator codes to diagnostic events. These are separate from your clock settings and background images.

Support. If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you send so that we can respond. Support emails are retained for 30 days. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or purchase tokens.

Access and deletion. You can clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove its local app data. Separately exported files and device backups are managed through the services and settings where they are stored. To request deletion of your support emails and attachments, contact tecentmoon.feedback@gmail.com. We will process your request as soon as possible after receiving it. Please identify the correspondence concerned without sending payment-card details or purchase tokens. Google account, payment and purchase records are managed by Google; contact Google or use your Google account controls for those records.

Children and families. The basic clock does not require a Super Clock account, name, date of birth or contact details. The app has no in-app chat or advertising integration. Google Play manages purchases and redemption; parents and guardians can use Google Play's purchase approval and parental controls. Children should ask a parent or guardian before making a purchase or contacting support and should not send personal information in a support message. A parent or guardian can contact the privacy email above about information sent to us. The Google services and diagnostic processing described above also apply when those services are used.

Changes. We will update this page and its effective date when this policy changes, and provide notice or request consent where required.
